    From 10s Phone Video to Collision-Checked 3D Twin

    1. 1. Multi-view capture & privacy blur: 10.4 s iPhone 0.5× walkthrough (52 frames @ 5 fps); attendee faces & background blurred via macOS Apple Vision.
    2. 2. Local pycolmap sparse 3D: SIFT feature matching + bundle adjustment on Mac CPU solves 52/52 camera poses and 20,762 sparse 3D points (3,456 filtered starting points with <3 px error).
    3. 3. Colab T4 GPU gsplat (68k → 17k Gaussians): Open-source gsplat 1.5.3 custom CUDA kernels on an NVIDIA Tesla T4 GPU optimize 3D Gaussian ellipsoids (1,500 steps in 10.0 s) into 68,192 Gaussians, cropped to 17,157 desk Gaussians (desk.splat) + 943 Celsius-can Gaussians.
    4. 4. RANSAC plane fit → MuJoCo collision boxes: 2,000-iteration RANSAC fits the tabletop frame and 5 rigid screen/laptop boxes (1.6–3.7 mm RMS, +8 mm margin) so MuJoCo measures real arm-screen contact force (N) and penetration (mm).
